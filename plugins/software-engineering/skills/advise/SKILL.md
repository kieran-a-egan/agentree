# Advise

## Inputs

- **question** (required): the decision or blocker.
- **evidence** (required): what was tried and observed, with results.
- **options** (optional): the candidates already considered.

## Procedure

1. Check the evidence before the conclusion; say if it is insufficient.
2. Name the constraint that decides between options.
3. Give one recommendation, not a survey.

## Outputs

A reply with:
- **Recommendation**: the one thing to do next.
- **Reasoning**: the deciding constraint and the evidence behind it.
- **Risks**: what could make this wrong and how to notice.

## Limits

Advice only: never edits production code. The lead decides; consequential choices still go to the human when the run's policy says so.
